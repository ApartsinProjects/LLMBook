Section 36.4

Models

Big Picture

Pre-trained and open-weight models for retrieval and information extraction.

Models are the substrate. This section reviews the major open-weight and proprietary models relevant to retrieval and information extraction, their licensing terms, hardware requirements, and the criteria for choosing between them.

Big Picture

Model selection in retrieval and information extraction balances capability (does it work?), licensing (can we deploy it?), cost (can we afford to serve it?), and ecosystem (do tools support it?).
