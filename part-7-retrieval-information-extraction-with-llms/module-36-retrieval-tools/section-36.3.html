Section 36.3

Datasets and Benchmarks

Big Picture

Public datasets and benchmarks for retrieval and information extraction.

Benchmarks anchor progress in retrieval and information extraction. This section curates the canonical public datasets, the academic benchmarks driving leaderboards, and the practical evaluation suites that practitioners use to gate releases.

Warning

Public benchmarks suffer from contamination (training data leakage) and ceiling effects. Production teams should maintain held-out internal benchmarks alongside public ones.
