External Reading & Communities

Section 25.5

RAG is heavily blogged. The signal-to-noise is poor; the resources below are the ones that survive scrutiny.

25.5.1 Foundational papers

  • Lewis et al., "Retrieval-Augmented Generation" (2020): the original RAG paper.
  • Gao et al., "Retrieval-Augmented Generation for LLMs: A Survey" (2024): the most comprehensive survey.
  • Asai et al., "Self-RAG" (2023): self-reflective RAG.
  • Karpukhin et al., "Dense Passage Retrieval" (2020): the DPR paper, foundational for modern retrievers.

25.5.2 Tutorials and recipes

  • Anthropic "Contextual Retrieval" (Sep 2024): THE production-RAG 2024-25 reference; prepend a short AI-generated document context to each chunk before embedding and watch retrieval failure rates fall.
  • LangChain RAG tutorial.
  • LlamaIndex documentation: especially the "Building Performant RAG Applications" guides.
  • Haystack docs and the deepset blog.
  • Eugene Yan, "LLM Patterns": production-RAG advice from someone who has shipped it.
  • Jina AI engineering blog: multilingual retrieval research and embedding-model deep dives.
  • Pinecone "RAG learning center": vendor docs but often cited and well-written.
  • Cameron Wolfe's "Information Retrieval for LLMs" series (2024): practitioner-focused; pairs with the academic surveys.
  • Wang et al., "Searching for Best Practices in Retrieval-Augmented Generation" (2024): empirical survey of what actually works; sits alongside the Gao 2024 survey.

25.5.3 Communities

  • r/Rag: production RAG questions.
  • LangChain Discord and LlamaIndex Discord (linked from their docs).
  • HF forums for embedding-model-specific questions.
A test for RAG advice

If a blog post recommends a "magic chunk size" (the "always use 512 tokens" or "1024 with 200 overlap" claims you will encounter) or a single best embedding model, treat it skeptically. Both depend on the corpus, the query distribution, and the downstream LLM. The only universal advice is "measure, do not guess."

The RAG vs long-context debate

Since Gemini 1.5 shipped a 1M-token context in 2024, the "RAG is dead, just stuff the whole corpus in context" thread recurs on AI Twitter and Bluesky every few months. The honest answer in 2026: long context is a useful complement when corpus < 1M tokens and queries are diverse, but it is not a substitute for retrieval at corpus scale (cost, latency, and lost-in-the-middle failure modes all hurt). RAGTruth-style benchmarks show retrieval-augmented systems still outperform pure long-context on factuality for large corpora.
