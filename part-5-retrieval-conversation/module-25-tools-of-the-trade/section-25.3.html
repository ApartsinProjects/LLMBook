Datasets & Benchmarks

Section 25.3

RAG datasets fall into three categories: retrieval-quality benchmarks (does my embedding find the right chunk?), end-to-end QA benchmarks (does the system answer correctly?), and the embedding-training corpora behind sentence-transformers and BGE.

25.3.1 Retrieval benchmarks

  • BEIR: heterogeneous IR benchmark across 18 datasets. The standard retrieval benchmark.
  • MTEB: Massive Text Embedding Benchmark from Hugging Face. Covers 56 tasks across 7 domains.
  • MS MARCO: web-passage retrieval; the canonical training set for dense retrievers.

25.3.2 End-to-end RAG / QA benchmarks

  • SQuAD 2.0: extractive QA over Wikipedia paragraphs.
  • Natural Questions: real Google search queries with Wikipedia answers.
  • HotpotQA: multi-hop QA requiring reasoning over multiple documents.
  • MultiSpanQA: longer, multi-span answers.
  • RAGAS: LLM-as-judge framework for evaluating RAG output on custom datasets; not a dataset itself but the way most production teams evaluate.

25.3.3 Embedding-training corpora

  • SBERT training data: triplets and pairs for contrastive embedding training.
  • Hugging Face embedding-training-data: aggregated mix used for many open embedding models.

25.3.4 Comparing the datasets

Eval RAG, not just retrieval

BEIR and MTEB measure embedding quality. RAGAS-style end-to-end eval measures whether the final answer is grounded and correct. The two metrics frequently diverge: a system can have great recall and bad answers, or vice versa. Always measure both.
