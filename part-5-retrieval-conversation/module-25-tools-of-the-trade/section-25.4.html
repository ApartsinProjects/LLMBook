Models

Section 25.4

Part V uses two model categories: embedding models (which encode chunks into vectors) and reranker models (which re-score candidates after first-stage retrieval). The LLMs that generate the final answer were covered in Chapter 12; this section focuses on the retrieval-specific models.

25.4.1 Open-source embedding models

  • BGE family from BAAI: bge-large-en-v1.5 and bge-m3 (multilingual). The open SOTA on MTEB for much of 2024-2025.
  • E5 family from Microsoft: e5-large-v2 and the multilingual variants.
  • Snowflake Arctic Embed: strong on English retrieval.
  • Nomic Embed: open-source with Matryoshka representations.
  • all-MiniLM-L6-v2: 22M parameters, the default "good enough and fast" embedding.

25.4.2 API-based embeddings

  • OpenAI text-embedding-3-large / -small.
  • Cohere Embed v3: best off-the-shelf multilingual.
  • Voyage AI: specialized domain models (voyage-code, voyage-law).
  • Google Gemini Embedding.

25.4.3 Reranker models

  • BGE Reranker v2-m3: open multilingual reranker.
  • Cohere Rerank 3: managed reranker; near-SOTA quality.
  • Jina Reranker v2: open multilingual.
  • mxbai-rerank from Mixedbread: open SOTA on several reranker benchmarks.

Reranking is the cheap win

The largest quality lift in most RAG pipelines comes not from a better embedding model but from adding a reranker. A two-stage system (cheap embedding -> top-100 -> rerank to top-5) routinely outperforms a single-stage system with the best available embeddings, at a small fraction of the cost.
