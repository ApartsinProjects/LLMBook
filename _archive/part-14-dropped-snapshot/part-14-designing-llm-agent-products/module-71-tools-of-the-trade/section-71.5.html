External Reading & Communities

Section 71.5

Part X's literature is product, not ML. The references below are the canonical product- and startup-engineering reads as filtered for AI-product work.

71.5.1 Books worth owning

  • Marty Cagan, Inspired.
  • Ryan Singer, Shape Up (free online).
  • Lenny's Newsletter: PM career and tactics.

71.5.2 AI-product-specific blogs

  • Eugene Yan.
  • Hamel Husain: "Your AI Product Needs Evals" (2024) is the most-cited essay on building AI-product evals.
  • Simon Willison's Weblog.
  • Latent Space podcast + newsletter, plus the annual State of AI Engineering survey from swyx.
  • Every's Chain-of-Thought.
  • AI Engineer World's Fair: the dominant 2024-25 AI-engineering conference; talks on YouTube.
  • The "12-factor agents" community standard (2024-25): the emerging agent-engineering doctrine, modeled on Heroku's 12-factor app pattern.

71.5.3 Communities

  • Hacker News: launch coverage and tech debate.
  • Indie Hackers: revenue-first AI-builder community.
  • Product Hunt: launch venue.
