Platforms

Section 39.1

Part IX's platforms divide into moderation APIs, red-team platforms, and policy / compliance services.

39.1.1 Moderation APIs

  • OpenAI Moderation API: free, low-latency text classifier across 13 categories.
  • Anthropic safety classifiers (integrated, not a separate endpoint).
  • Azure AI Content Safety: classifier service across text and image.
  • Google Cloud Natural Language safety.

39.1.2 Red-team and adversarial-testing platforms

  • Garak: open-source LLM vulnerability scanner from NVIDIA.
  • PyRIT: Microsoft's open red-teaming toolkit.
  • Haize Labs: commercial automated red-team platform.
  • Giskard: ML model and LLM testing platform.

39.1.3 Compliance and governance

  • Credo AI, Fairly AI: AI governance SaaS for compliance reporting (NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act).
  • ModelBench: enterprise model evaluation with policy mapping.
