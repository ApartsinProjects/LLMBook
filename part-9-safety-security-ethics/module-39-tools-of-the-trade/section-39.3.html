Datasets & Benchmarks

Section 39.3

Safety datasets cover three areas: harmful-output benchmarks, jailbreak / adversarial-input corpora, and bias / fairness benchmarks.

39.3.1 Harmful-output benchmarks

  • HarmBench: 400 harmful behaviors across 7 categories.
  • AdvBench: 520 adversarial prompts and harmful strings.
  • JailbreakBench: standardized jailbreak evaluation.
  • Anthropic HH-RLHF: helpful/harmless preference pairs.

39.3.2 Bias and fairness

  • Anthropic Global Opinions: cross-cultural bias measurement.
  • BBQ: bias benchmark for QA.
  • Winogender: gender-bias schema.
  • Anthropic discrim-eval: discrimination measurement.

39.3.3 Truthfulness and hallucination

  • TruthfulQA.
  • HaluEval: hallucination benchmark.
  • MT-Bench: multi-turn quality including truthfulness probes.

39.3.4 Comparing the datasets

Adversarial datasets age fast

Jailbreak benchmarks are arms races. A model that "passes" the 2023 jailbreak set may fail current attacks. Always pair public benchmarks with live red-team campaigns using Garak or PyRIT.
