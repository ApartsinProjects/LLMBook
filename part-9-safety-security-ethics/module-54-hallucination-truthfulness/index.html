Hallucination and Truthfulness

"The most dangerous output is the one that sounds right but is not."

— A Pragmatic Educator

Chapter Overview

Hallucination is the failure mode that makes LLMs unsafe for high-stakes use even when they are accurate most of the time. This chapter covers the taxonomy of hallucinations, the detection and mitigation patterns that work in production, and the calibration and uncertainty-quantification techniques that let a model decline to answer when it does not know.

Big Picture

Detection, grounding via RAG, calibrated abstention, and hallucination evaluation.
