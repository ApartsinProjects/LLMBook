Frontier Safety and Open Problems

"The hardest problems in AI safety are the ones we have not yet learned to name."

— A Pragmatic Educator

Chapter Overview

This chapter looks at the safety problems that frontier capability advances create faster than we solve them: deceptive alignment, capability overhang, multi-agent emergent dynamics, and the governance frameworks that try to keep pace. It is the open-research chapter, written so engineers can contribute to the active debates rather than just consume the latest preprint.

Big Picture

Alignment research, frontier capability evaluations, compute governance, mechanistic interpretability, and open problems.
