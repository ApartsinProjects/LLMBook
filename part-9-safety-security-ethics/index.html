Part Overview

Part IX addresses the safety, security, ethical, and regulatory dimensions of LLM deployment. You will learn to harden systems against adversarial attacks, detect and mitigate hallucinations and bias, navigate the evolving regulatory landscape (including EU AI Act compliance), implement red teaming programs, defend agentic systems against prompt-injection and supply-chain attacks, and stand up a working guardrails stack.

Chapters: 3 (Chapters 37, 38, and 39). Useful for anyone shipping LLM applications to real users.

Big Picture

Technical excellence means little without responsible deployment. Part IX addresses the safety, security, ethical, and regulatory dimensions of LLM and agent adoption, with dedicated chapters on safety/ethics/regulation, agent safety and security, and the guardrails toolchain you actually deploy.
