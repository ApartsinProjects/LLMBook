LLM Product Management

Specs, evals, and the AI product manager's playbook.

Big Picture

Once a real problem is in your hands, the work shifts from "is there a there there?" to "how do we ship the smallest thing that proves it?". This chapter is the AI product manager's playbook: turning a hypothesis into a spec, designing evals as a first-class artifact, and running the iteration loop on a probabilistic system.

Two industry artifacts now serve as reference specs: Anthropic's Claude evaluation methodology (2024) and OpenAI's Model Spec (May 2024) show what a defensible AI product spec looks like in practice. The cautionary counter-case is Air Canada's chatbot ruling (Feb 2024), where a Civil Resolution Tribunal held the airline liable for a bereavement-fare promise its support bot invented: a spec gap that became legal liability.
