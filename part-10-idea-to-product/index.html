Part X: Idea to Product

The entrepreneur's operating model for building, launching, and iterating on AI-centered products.

"Vision without execution is hallucination."

Thomas Edison

Part Overview

Part X bridges the gap between knowing how LLMs work and shipping a product that uses them. Where earlier parts taught you to build RAG pipelines, fine-tune models, and orchestrate agents, this part shows you how to run the rapid, evidence-driven iteration cycles that turn a promising prototype into a reliable, economically viable product. It is designed for founders, product managers, and engineers who need an operating model, not just technical skills.

Chapters: 11 (Chapters 40 through 50). The arc runs ideation, product management, strategy and use-case prioritization, prototyping via vibe-coding, building an MVP, the founder's prototype-to-MVP loop, compute and infrastructure planning, scaling economics and ROI, shipping and scaling, post-launch monitoring, and closes with the idea-to-product toolkit.

Big Picture

AI product development is fundamentally different from deterministic software development. Outputs are probabilistic, quality requires measurement across distributions, and "shipping" means establishing a quality gate with continuous monitoring. This part gives you the operating model to navigate these differences: from framing your AI hypothesis, through responsible prototyping with AI coding tools, to launch readiness with real unit economics, provider portability, and post-launch iteration.
