BUILDING CONVERSATIONAL AI WITH LLMS AND AGENTS

A 35-chapter LLM textbook that takes you from PyTorch fundamentals to shipping autonomous agents. ~800 pages of theory, working code, and production engineering, written for engineers, researchers, and students who need to ship real LLM systems.

If you have spent the last year learning LLMs from scattered blog posts, GitHub READMEs, and outdated tutorials, this book is for you. Every chapter answers a specific question someone shipping LLM-powered systems has actually had to answer: how attention works, how to fine-tune a model on your data, how to build an agent that uses tools without going off the rails, how to evaluate the thing you just built.

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

Foundations (Ch 0-5): PyTorch, NLP, tokenization, sequence models, the Transformer architecture from scratch, and decoding strategies.

Understanding LLMs (Ch 6-10): pre-training, scaling laws, the modern model landscape, reasoning and test-time compute, inference optimization, and mechanistic interpretability.

Working with LLMs (Ch 11-13): APIs and structured output, prompt engineering, and hybrid ML+LLM architectures.

Training and Adapting (Ch 14-17): synthetic data, fine-tuning, PEFT (LoRA, QLoRA, DoRA), distillation, model merging, and alignment with RLHF, DPO, and RLVR.

Retrieval and Conversation (Ch 18-20): embeddings, vector databases, semantic search, retrieval-augmented generation, and conversational AI.

Agentic AI (Ch 21-25): agent foundations, tool use (function calling, MCP, A2A), multi-agent systems, specialized agents (code, browser, research), and agent safety in production.

AI Applications (Ch 26-27): multimodal models (vision, audio, video) and domain-specific apps in healthcare, finance, legal, code, and robotics.

Evaluation and Production (Ch 28-29): LLM evaluation, observability, LLMOps, and deployment engineering.

Safety and Strategy (Ch 30-31): safety, ethics, regulation (red teaming, EU AI Act), and the strategy, product management, and ROI of LLM adoption.

Frontiers (Ch 33): emerging architectures (Mamba, SSMs), scaling frontiers, world models, mechanistic interpretability at scale, and a 2026 retrospective on what the field settled and what remains open.

From Idea to AI Product (Ch 34-35): turning an idea into a product hypothesis, the observe-steer development loop, and shipping and scaling AI products to users.

WHO THIS BOOK IS FOR

Software engineers, ML practitioners, researchers, product leaders, domain specialists, and educators. Familiarity with Python is assumed; appendices cover the mathematical and ML prerequisites for readers who need them.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Eight goal-based reading pathways for RAG engineers, agent builders, ML practitioners moving to LLMs, researchers, safety specialists, founders and PMs, instructors, and curious generalists.

Course-ready: five complete syllabi covering undergraduate engineering, undergraduate research, graduate engineering, graduate research, and professional bootcamp tracks.

28 appendices: Mathematical Foundations, ML Essentials, Python, environment setup, Git, the Glossary, hardware and compute, model cards, prompt templates, datasets and benchmarks, framework guides (HuggingFace, LangChain with LlamaIndex/LangGraph/CrewAI/Semantic Kernel/DSPy coverage), infrastructure (W&B, MLflow, vLLM, TGI, SGLang, Ray, PySpark, Docker), seven industry-specific guides (Legal, Finance, Healthcare, Education, Cybersecurity, Government, Manufacturing), and three cross-cutting reference catalogs (Master Reference Tables, Production Patterns Reference, Pedagogy Kit).

Up to date for 2026: reasoning models (o1/o3, DeepSeek-R1), test-time compute scaling, modern PEFT, agentic protocols (MCP, A2A, AG-UI), multimodal foundation models, FlashAttention-3, PagedAttention, RLVR, and the production patterns that 2026 settled.

Every chapter has at least one hands-on lab. Learn by building, not just by reading.

End-to-end capstone project (4-6 weeks): design, build, evaluate, and present a production-grade LLM application that integrates every major skill from the book.
