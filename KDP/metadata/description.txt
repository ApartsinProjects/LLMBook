BUILDING CONVERSATIONAL AI WITH LLMS AND AGENTS

From the mathematics of attention to production agent systems. A practitioner's guide for engineers, researchers, and students.

Whether you want to build your first RAG pipeline, ship an AI agent to production, or make strategic decisions about LLM adoption at your organization, this comprehensive textbook meets you where you are. Across 39 chapters in 11 parts, plus 22 appendices covering frameworks and prerequisites, the book takes you from first principles through cutting-edge agentic systems, with hands-on labs, runnable code, and production-grade engineering practices throughout.

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

Foundations: PyTorch and ML essentials, NLP and text representation, tokenization, sequence models, the Transformer architecture, and decoding strategies.

Understanding LLMs: pre-training and scaling laws, the modern model landscape, reasoning and test-time compute, inference optimization, and mechanistic interpretability.

Working with LLMs: APIs, prompt engineering, and hybrid ML+LLM architectures.

Training and adaptation: synthetic data generation, fine-tuning fundamentals, PEFT (LoRA, QLoRA), distillation, model merging, and alignment with RLHF and DPO.

Retrieval and conversation: embeddings, vector databases, semantic search, retrieval-augmented generation, and conversational AI systems.

Agentic AI: agent foundations, tool use and function calling, multi-agent systems, specialized agents, and agent safety in production.

Multimodal and applications: vision-language models and real-world LLM applications.

Production: evaluation, observability, monitoring, and engineering for LLM systems at scale.

Safety and strategy: alignment, ethics, regulation, product strategy, and ROI analysis.

Frontiers: emerging architectures and the broader impact of AI on society.

From idea to product: shipping, scaling, and steering LLM-powered products.

WHO THIS BOOK IS FOR

Software engineers, ML practitioners, researchers, product leaders, domain specialists, and educators. Familiarity with Python is assumed; appendices cover the mathematical and ML prerequisites for readers who need them.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Twenty tailored reading pathways help you find the most relevant chapters for goals like RAG engineering, agent building, ML-to-LLM transition, or product leadership.

Course-ready: nine syllabi for graduate, undergraduate, and specialist tracks.

Framework appendices for Hugging Face, LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, LlamaIndex, Semantic Kernel, DSPy, plus environment setup, distributed ML, inference serving, and Docker.

Up to date for 2026: covers reasoning models, test-time compute, modern PEFT, and agentic protocols.
