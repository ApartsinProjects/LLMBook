BUILDING CONVERSATIONAL AI WITH LLMS AND AGENTS

The 39-chapter LLM textbook that takes you from PyTorch fundamentals to shipping autonomous agents. 800+ pages of theory, code, and production engineering, written for engineers, researchers, and students who actually need to ship working LLM systems.

If you have spent the last year learning LLMs from scattered blog posts, GitHub READMEs, and outdated tutorials, this book is for you. Every chapter answers a specific question someone shipping LLM-powered systems has actually had to answer: how attention works, how to fine-tune a model on your data, how to build an agent that uses tools without going off the rails, how to evaluate the thing you just built.

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

Foundations (Ch 0-5): PyTorch, NLP, tokenization, sequence models, the Transformer architecture from scratch, and decoding strategies.

Understanding LLMs (Ch 6-9, 18): pre-training, scaling laws, the modern model landscape, reasoning and test-time compute, inference optimization, and mechanistic interpretability.

Working with LLMs (Ch 10-12): APIs, prompt engineering, and hybrid ML+LLM architectures.

Training and adaptation (Ch 13-17): synthetic data, fine-tuning, PEFT (LoRA, QLoRA), distillation, model merging, and alignment with RLHF and DPO.

Retrieval and conversation (Ch 19-21): embeddings, vector databases, semantic search, retrieval-augmented generation, and conversational AI.

Agentic AI (Ch 22-26): agent foundations, tool use, multi-agent systems, specialized agents, and agent safety in production.

Multimodal, applications, production (Ch 27-31): vision-language models, real-world apps, evaluation, observability, and engineering at scale.

Safety, strategy, frontiers, product (Ch 32-38): alignment, ethics, regulation, ROI, emerging architectures, and shipping LLM-powered products.

WHO THIS BOOK IS FOR

Software engineers, ML practitioners, researchers, product leaders, domain specialists, and educators. Familiarity with Python is assumed; appendices cover the mathematical and ML prerequisites for readers who need them.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Twenty tailored reading pathways for RAG engineers, agent builders, ML-to-LLM transitioners, product leaders, and instructors.

Course-ready: nine syllabi for graduate, undergraduate, and specialist tracks. Free instructor copies and slide decks available.

22 framework appendices covering Hugging Face, LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, LlamaIndex, Semantic Kernel, DSPy, plus environment setup, distributed ML, inference serving, and Docker.

Up to date for 2026: reasoning models, test-time compute, modern PEFT, agentic protocols (MCP, A2A), and multimodal foundation models.

Every chapter has a hands-on lab. Learn by building, not just by reading.
