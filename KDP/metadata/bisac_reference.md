# BISAC Categories Notes

## What BISAC is

BISAC (Book Industry Standards and Communications) is the standard taxonomy used by booksellers (including Amazon, Barnes & Noble, retail bookstores, libraries) to classify books. Every book on Amazon has at least one BISAC code, and KDP submission requires you to select your category from a hierarchical browser whose leaves correspond to BISAC codes.

## How KDP categories work in 2026

KDP changed its category interface in 2023; you no longer paste BISAC codes directly. Instead, you select from a hierarchical browser:

1. In the KDP "Categories" step, click "Choose categories"
2. Browse the tree (e.g., Nonfiction > Computers > Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence)
3. Pick **up to 3 categories** (was 2 prior to 2023)
4. The selected categories map internally to BISAC codes

## Tip on category strategy

- KDP picks one category as **primary** and uses it for the "Best Sellers Rank" calculation. Choose the one with **highest match + lowest competition** (smaller subcategories = easier to rank).
- "AI & Semantics" is highly competitive in 2026. If your goal is to crack a Top-100 Best Sellers list, consider a narrower secondary like "Programming Algorithms" or "Software Development > Tools" as primary instead.
- You can request additional/specialized categories after publication via [Amazon KDP support](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201729280).
