## Executive Summary

Total items checked: **~1,860 assets** (4 embedded fonts + 60 KaTeX fonts as build dependency, 681 source images, 1,700+ code blocks sampled, 649 unique arXiv references, ~58 DOI references, 38+ trademark mentions).

Items requiring authors' attention before submission: **6 items** (1 RED, 5 YELLOW). The book is in strong shape overall: fonts are SIL-OFL licensed, code samples are original, bibliography uses scholarly references with no long verbatim quotes, no trademark misuse, and the cover is AI-generated by the authors. The single RED issue is the **absence of a formal copyright/legal page** in the front matter. Three figures reproduce diagrams from third-party blog posts (Raschka, Lambert, Huyen, NVIDIA, Lilian Weng) with attribution but **no documented permission**, which is the second priority.

### Top 3 fixes before clicking "Publish"

1. **Add a copyright / legal-disclaimer / trademark-notice page** to the front matter. Currently the only copyright assertion lives in `front-matter/index.html` footer ("&copy; 2026 ..."). KDP requires (and good practice expects) a dedicated copyright page with rights statement, disclaimer, and trademark acknowledgements.
2. **Resolve the third-party diagram reproductions.** Either (a) replace the four flagged figures with original redraws, or (b) document permission emails / confirm fair-use rationale in writing. Files: `lora-weights-raschka.png`, `raschka-bpe-overview.jpg`, the NVIDIA RAG diagram in 20.1.3, the Lambert/Huyen RLHF diagrams in 17.1.2, and the Lilian Weng diffusion diagram in 27.1.3.
3. **Confirm `cover_kdp.jpg` is the final cover.** `cover_notes.md` flags the current file as a placeholder; the Gemini-generated artwork variants should be promoted before submission, and the authors should retain the Gemini generation logs as proof of authorship per Amazon's AI-content disclosure policy.

## Final summary

The book is in remarkably good shape for KDP submission. Embedded fonts are SIL OFL (free for commercial reuse). The 1,700+ code blocks sampled are original (or trivially short canonical API-usage that falls under fair use). Bibliography contains 649 unique arXiv references and 58 DOIs but no quotations long enough to require permission. Trademarks are used nominatively and never as marketing claims. The cover and most illustrations are AI-generated by the authors.

Two issues block a clean submission:

1. **RED — Missing copyright page.** The book has only a footer-line copyright assertion. KDP best practice (and protection against frivolous infringement claims) requires a dedicated front-matter page combining (a) copyright notice, (b) "all rights reserved" / no-reproduction clause, (c) "as-is" disclaimer of warranty, (d) trademark acknowledgement of OpenAI/Anthropic/Google/Hugging Face/PyTorch marks, and (e) attribution for Source Serif 4, Source Code Pro, and KaTeX. A template is supplied in Section 6 above.

2. **YELLOW — Six third-party diagrams** are reproduced with attribution but no documented permission (Raschka LoRA, Raschka BPE, NVIDIA RAG, Lambert/Huggingface RLHF, Huyen RLHF, Lilian Weng diffusion, Edge et al. GraphRAG). Recommended action: redraw each as an original Mermaid or schematic figure. The conceptual content is well-documented elsewhere; the figures themselves are not load-bearing.

3. **YELLOW — Placeholder cover.** `cover_notes.md` flags `cover_kdp.jpg` as a placeholder pending a decision between Gemini-generated variants. Promote the chosen final and rebuild before submission.

All other categories pass without action: fonts, code, bibliography, trademark usage, KDP metadata, DRM/AI-disclosure flags.
