What You Will Learn

Build production AI agents from first principles. 35 chapters across 11 parts, plus 22 framework appendices. From PyTorch fundamentals through autonomous agent systems shipped to production.

Part I · Foundations
PyTorch & Transformers from scratch
Build a 300-line decoder-only Transformer in Chapter 04. Train it. Watch it generate text.
Part II · Understanding LLMs
Inside the black box
Pretraining and scaling laws, modern model landscape, reasoning & test-time compute, inference optimization, and the interpretability tools that explain model behavior.
Part III · Working with LLMs
APIs, prompts, hybrid systems
Provider APIs, prompt engineering as a craft, and decision frameworks for when to combine classical ML with LLMs.
Part IV · Training & Adapting
Fine-tuning, PEFT, alignment
LoRA, QLoRA, distillation, model merging. RLHF and DPO. Synthetic data generation.
Part V · Retrieval & Conversation
RAG and conversational AI
Embeddings, vector databases, semantic search. Build a complete RAG pipeline.
Part VI · Agentic AI
Tool use, multi-agent, safety
Agent foundations. MCP and A2A protocols. Specialized agents. Production safety.
Part VII · AI Applications
Multimodal & industry deep-dives
Vision-language models, audio & video generation, embodied agents. Vibe-coding, finance, healthcare, recommendation, cybersecurity.
Part VIII · Evaluation & Production
Quality metrics & LLMOps
Evaluation that catches what tests miss. Observability, deployment, AI gateways, edge inference, and reliability engineering.
Part IX · Safety & Strategy
Safety, regulation, and ROI
EU AI Act compliance, red-teaming, privacy attacks, hallucination defense. Vendor evaluation, build-vs-buy, and unit economics.
Part XII · Frontiers
Where the field is going
Emergent abilities, alternative architectures, world models, scaling frontiers, and the open theoretical questions about reasoning and agency.
Part X · From Idea to AI Product
Idea → prototype → ship
The observe-steer development loop, the founder's prototype loop, and post-launch monitoring for AI-native products.
Appendices A – AF
28 framework + reference appendices
Hugging Face, LangChain (with LangGraph, CrewAI, LlamaIndex, Semantic Kernel, DSPy), Docker, vLLM, plus 7 industry guides and 3 cross-cutting reference catalogs.
Capstone Project
End-to-end LLM system (4-6 weeks)
Design, build, evaluate, and present a production-grade LLM application that integrates every major skill from the book. Start here →
