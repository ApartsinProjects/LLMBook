Section 56.3

Datasets and Benchmarks

Big Picture

Public datasets and benchmarks for responsible AI and governance.

Benchmarks anchor progress in responsible AI and governance. This section curates the canonical public datasets, the academic benchmarks driving leaderboards, and the practical evaluation suites that practitioners use to gate releases.

Warning

Public benchmarks suffer from contamination (training data leakage) and ceiling effects. Production teams should maintain held-out internal benchmarks alongside public ones.
