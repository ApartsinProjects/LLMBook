External Reading & Communities

Section 60.5

The list below is a starting point per industry, not a survey.

60.5.1 Industry-specific reads

  • Medical: NEJM AI, JAMA's AI sections, Health Affairs, Lancet Digital Health, and Stat News.
  • Legal: Law360, Legaltech News, Artificial Lawyer, LegalTech Hub.
  • Finance: eFinancialCareers, The Trade, plus the BankAI, FinSight, and FintechAI events and newsletters.
  • Education: EdSurge, plus Princeton CITP's AI Snake Oil for critical-perspective on educational AI claims.
  • Cyber: Krebs on Security, Dark Reading.
  • Industry Slack/Discord communities: Pavilion (legal), HLTH (healthcare), RSAC (cybersecurity) host the largest practitioner networks.
Vertical AI standards are starting to matter

The NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 and sector-specific customizations (FDA AI/ML guidance for medical, SR 11-7 for financial models, NIST 800-218 for software supply chain) are increasingly cited in 2025-26 procurement processes. The framework you pick (or your vendor picks) affects which platforms are deployable; flag this when evaluating vendors.

60.5.2 Cross-industry AI venues

  • State of AI Report: annual market overview.
  • Stanford AI Index.
  • AI Alliance: open-source-friendly industry consortium.
