External Reading & Communities

Section 60.5

Industry-specific AI literature is fragmented across trade journals, vendor blogs, and academic conferences. The list below is a starting point per industry.

60.5.1 Industry-specific reads

  • Medical: NEJM AI, JAMA's AI sections.
  • Legal: Law360, Legaltech News.
  • Finance: eFinancialCareers, The Trade.
  • Education: EdSurge.
  • Cyber: Krebs on Security, Dark Reading.

60.5.2 Cross-industry AI venues

  • State of AI Report: annual market overview.
  • Stanford AI Index.
  • AI Alliance: open-source-friendly industry consortium.
