60.1.1 Legal

  • Harvey: GPT-based legal copilot; OpenAI partnership.
  • Hebbia: long-document analysis for finance and law.
  • CoCounsel (Casetext, Thomson Reuters).
  • Lexis+ AI.
  • Even.ai and Legora (2025 entrants): legal-vertical AI products competing with Harvey for mid-market firms.
  • Ironclad: AI for legal contracts and CLM (contract lifecycle management).

60.1.2 Finance

  • BloombergGPT: in-house Bloomberg LLM.
  • Hebbia: equity research and diligence; major 2024-25 product updates.
  • AlphaSense: market intelligence with embedded LLMs.
  • Kensho (S&P Global).
  • Rogo and Linq Alpha: 2024-25 finance-AI entrants competing with Hebbia for investment-banking workflows.

60.1.3 Healthcare

  • Med-PaLM (Google).
  • Nuance DAX Copilot (Microsoft).
  • Abridge: clinical-conversation summarization.
  • Hippocratic AI.
  • Ambience Healthcare and Suki AI: 2024-25 clinical voice-scribe products competing with DAX.
HIPAA Business Associate Agreement availability is the vendor-selection question

For healthcare deployments, the binding question is which API providers offer a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA). As of mid-2026: Anthropic via AWS Bedrock, Azure OpenAI, and GCP Healthcare data path all support BAAs. Direct API tiers from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google typically do not without enterprise contracts. Check your contract before designing on assumed compliance; one missing BAA invalidates the entire deployment plan.

60.1.5 Cyber, code, and others

  • Microsoft Security Copilot.

Underlying model vs vertical wrapper

Most "vertical AI" products are LangChain-style wrappers over frontier APIs with a curated knowledge base, vertical-specific eval, and SOC2 / HIPAA / FINRA compliance work. The defensible moat is data, integration, and compliance, not the model.
