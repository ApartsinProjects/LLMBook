LLMs in Creative Industries

Music, video, design, marketing copy: the multimodal generation playbook for creative work.

Big Picture

Creative industries are the most visible application of generative AI to the general public. Every consumer who has used ChatGPT to draft a poem, asked Midjourney for a logo, or hummed a tune into Suno has experienced the same shift: the cost floor for producing something creative has collapsed, while the cost ceiling for producing something distinctively good has stayed exactly where it was. This chapter walks the working professional through the four creative sub-domains where LLMs and multimodal models have most reshaped the workflow (music, video, design, marketing copy), then connects back to the broader story of how creative production at scale has become a hybrid human-AI discipline rather than a pure-AI replacement. The chapter complements the technical multimodal foundations in Part VII by focusing on what production teams actually ship, not just what models can do.

Real-World Scenario: Coca-Cola Y3000 as Campaign-as-Concept

Coca-Cola's September 2023 launch of Y3000 Zero Sugar is the cleanest example of AI being not just a tool in a creative campaign but the campaign concept itself. The flavor was marketed as "co-created with artificial intelligence," with promotional artwork generated through the company's "Create Real Magic" platform (built in partnership with OpenAI and Stability AI). Consumers submitted prompts about their vision of the year 3000; AI rendered the results; the brand selected and refined the best for global launch across ten-plus markets. The lesson: the most successful AI-in-creative campaigns in 2023 to 2026 are not those that hide AI involvement but those that frame AI participation as part of the brand story. Heinz's "AI-generated ketchup" campaign with Rethink agency and Mattel's use of Midjourney for Hot Wheels concept design follow the same pattern.
