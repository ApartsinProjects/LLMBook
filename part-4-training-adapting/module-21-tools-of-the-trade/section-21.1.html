Platforms

Section 21.1

Training is the chapter of the book where compute is the protagonist. The platforms in this section are not Python imports; they are GPU rentals, distributed-training fabrics, and experiment-tracking SaaS dashboards.

21.1.1 Compute providers for training

For Part IV's training-scale workloads, the same provider list from Section 12.1 applies, but the price-performance ratio matters more. Lambda, RunPod, and Vast.ai dominate the bare-metal-GPU rental market. CoreWeave, Crusoe, and Nebius serve larger clusters with NVLink and InfiniBand interconnect. Modal and Together AI wrap GPU rental in serverless Python APIs that are friendlier for small teams.

For full pretraining, the hyperscalers (AWS, GCP, Azure) and TPU pods still win on raw scale. For fine-tuning, the specialists are almost always cheaper.

21.1.2 Distributed-training fabrics

  • Hugging Face Accelerate: the lightweight abstraction over DDP, FSDP, and DeepSpeed that most TRL scripts assume.
  • DeepSpeed: Microsoft's ZeRO-1/2/3 sharding implementation; the standard for fitting large models into less GPU memory.
  • PyTorch FSDP: native sharded-data-parallel; the modern default since FSDP2.
  • Megatron-LM: NVIDIA's tensor/pipeline-parallel reference for serious pretraining.
  • PyTorch Lightning: the "trainer that hides the loop"; still common in academic labs.

21.1.3 Experiment tracking

  • Weights & Biases: the de facto standard for ML experiment tracking. Free tier for personal use; SaaS or self-hosted for teams.
  • MLflow: open-source competitor; the right answer when you must self-host.
  • Neptune.ai: research-focused alternative with strong reproducibility primitives.
  • TensorBoard: still ubiquitous for local logging; integrates with everything above.

21.1.4 Comparing the platforms

Compute Lambda / RunPod Hourly fine-tuning runs You manage the stack
Compute Modal Serverless Python, easy ergonomics Less cluster control
Distributed DeepSpeed ZeRO-3 Fitting large models in less VRAM Slower than FSDP2 on small clusters
Distributed PyTorch FSDP2 Native PyTorch sharding Fewer config tutorials
Tracking W&B Default for teams Paid plans for private teams
Spot instances and checkpointing

Spot rentals are 50 to 70 percent cheaper but can vanish with two minutes notice. Always checkpoint to S3 (or equivalent) every N steps. Recovering a 24-hour fine-tune from scratch is a worse outcome than paying double.
