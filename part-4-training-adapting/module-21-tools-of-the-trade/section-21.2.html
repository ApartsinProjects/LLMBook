Libraries & Frameworks

Section 21.2

Part IV's training libraries split into three layers: the low-level engine (transformers + accelerate), the algorithm libraries (TRL, PEFT), and the high-level recipe layers (axolotl, lit-gpt). Pick a layer based on how much you want to write yourself.

21.2.1 The training engine

The training loop almost always wraps Hugging Face Trainer or its newer SFTTrainer subclass. Accelerate handles the distributed orchestration. The combination of transformers + accelerate + bitsandbytes is what every recipe in Part IV assumes.

bitsandbytes provides 8-bit and 4-bit quantization (NF4) for QLoRA. FlashAttention 3 handles the attention kernels that make long-context fine-tuning feasible on consumer GPUs.

21.2.2 Algorithm libraries

  • TRL: Hugging Face's preference-learning library. SFTTrainer, DPOTrainer, GRPOTrainer, PPOTrainer, RLOOTrainer. The single most-used implementation of RLHF and DPO in open-source as of 2026.
  • PEFT: LoRA, QLoRA, prefix tuning, IA3, prompt tuning. Adds a LoraConfig wrapper around any transformers model.
  • OpenRLHF: alternative RLHF framework with stronger Ray integration and DeepSpeed defaults.
  • verl: ByteDance's RL training framework, popular for GRPO and reasoning-model training.

21.2.3 Recipe layers

  • axolotl: YAML-config-driven fine-tuning. Wraps transformers + TRL + PEFT + Accelerate behind a single config file. Most open-source fine-tunes you see on Hugging Face were trained with axolotl.
  • lit-gpt (Lightning Studios): from-scratch GPT reimplementation aimed at clarity and pretraining; a good middle ground between nanoGPT and production frameworks.
  • Unsloth: 2x faster fine-tuning via custom Triton kernels; the right answer for tight-budget single-GPU LoRA work.
  • LLaMA-Factory: another YAML-driven layer with a Gradio UI; popular in Asia.

21.2.4 Comparing the libraries

21.2.1 Training libraries and their layers
Library Layer Owns When not to use
transformers Engine Architectures + Trainer base Custom pretraining from scratch
TRL Algorithm SFT, DPO, GRPO, PPO Heavyweight RL with Ray (use OpenRLHF)
PEFT Algorithm LoRA / QLoRA / adapters Full fine-tune
axolotl Recipe YAML-driven SFT/DPO Highly custom training loops
Unsloth Engine + Recipe Fast single-GPU LoRA Multi-GPU distributed

21.2.5 Practical defaults

For a single 24 GB GPU LoRA fine-tune, Unsloth is the fastest path. For multi-GPU SFT with a config you want to share, axolotl is the right answer. For RLHF or DPO research where you need to read the training loop, raw TRL is what published papers cite. Appendix J covers W&B and MLflow wiring.
