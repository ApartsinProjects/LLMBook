Datasets & Benchmarks

Section 21.3

Part IV uses three categories of dataset: instruction-tuning data (for SFT), preference data (for DPO/RLHF), and reasoning-trace data (for GRPO and reasoning fine-tunes). The list below covers the ones that show up in Chapters 17 through 20.

21.3.1 Instruction-tuning data

  • Alpaca: 52K instructions generated from text-davinci-003 by Stanford in 2023. The seminal open instruction set; mostly historical interest now but still a useful smoke test.
  • ShareGPT: 90K real ChatGPT conversations scraped in 2023. The basis of Vicuna; still a common SFT ingredient although its provenance is legally fraught.
  • No Robots: 10K human-written instructions from Hugging Face. Small but high quality.
  • Tulu 3 SFT: AllenAI's 1M+ example open instruction mix. The state of the art for open instruction tuning as of 2026.
  • OpenHermes-2.5: 1M-example open mix from Nous Research.

21.3.2 Pretraining and continued-pretraining data

  • FineWeb-Edu: ~1.3T tokens of quality-filtered web text; the open standard for continued pretraining and small-model training.
  • FineWeb: the unfiltered 15T-token parent.
  • The Stack v2: code corpus for code-specific continued pretraining.

21.3.3 Preference and reasoning data

  • UltraFeedback: 64K preference pairs, the most-used open DPO dataset.
  • Anthropic HH-RLHF: the original helpfulness and harmlessness preference dataset.
  • Capybara DPO: smaller curated DPO mix from Argilla.
  • OpenR1-Math-220k: reasoning traces in the DeepSeek-R1 style; used for GRPO and reasoning SFT.

21.3.5 Synthetic data

Many 2025-2026 fine-tunes use synthetic data generated by a stronger model. Tools to know: distilabel (Argilla) and NeMo-Curator (NVIDIA) for generating and filtering. Synthetic data raises licensing and contamination concerns covered properly in Part IX.

License hygiene

Training data licenses propagate to fine-tuned models. Alpaca's CC BY-NC restricts commercial use; ShareGPT's provenance is unclear. Always check the data card before training a model you intend to ship.
