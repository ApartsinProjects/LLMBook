## Slide 1 — Image Prompt Adapters
Title slide introducing IP-Adapter, a way to condition Stable Diffusion on reference images in the same way it is conditioned on text.

## Slide 2 — SD Denoiser with prompt conditioning
The slide diagrams the standard SD cross-attention path: text tokens are converted by the CLIP text encoder into keys and values, and pixel-token queries attend to them inside the UNet.

## Slide 3 — Image prompt for Stable Diffusion
IP-Adapter is described as a trained adapter that translates image patches into key and value vectors with the same semantics as text-prompt keys and values for UNet cross-attention. Training uses image-text pairs so the adapter learns the proper image encoding. The image is projected by a frozen CLIP image encoder followed by a small linear layer and layer-norm.

## Slide 4 — IP-adapter vs. Image2Image
A side-by-side comparison contrasts IP-Adapter with classical image-to-image. IP-Adapter conditions on the reference image at every UNet block via cross-attention, while image-to-image only initializes the latent and lets denoising drift away from it.

## Slide 5 — Example: Image Variants (no text prompt)
Three example outputs show that IP-Adapter alone (no text prompt) can produce variants of a reference image while preserving subject identity.

## Slide 6 — Example: Style Transfer
The slide describes pretrained style adapters that condition only on style and are applied to specific blocks (first up-sampling block after the bottleneck). Four panels show the same content rendered in different style references.

## Slide 7 — Combine ControlNet and IP-Adapter
Five panels show that IP-Adapter composes with ControlNet: a pose ControlNet locks the subject's posture, while IP-Adapter supplies the appearance from a reference image, yielding pose-controlled style transfer.

## Slide 8 — Specialized Face IP Adapter
A face-specialized IP-Adapter is trained on face-biased images with an additional face-embedding similarity loss, producing tighter identity preservation across generated portraits.

## Slide 9 — Training IP-adapter for style transfer
Section-header slide that transitions to the training recipe for style-transfer IP-Adapters.

## Slide 10 — Training data
The slide describes the data triplets: (text description, reference style image, target style image). A practical dataset is built by self-reconstruction (reference equals target), text descriptions generated by a captioning model, and data augmentation on reference and target to prevent pixel-copy memorization.

## Slide 11 — Training script in IP-Adapter repository
The slide shows the open-source IP-Adapter repository's training script and points to the relevant entry-point for users who want to train custom adapters.

## Deck-level takeaway
IP-Adapter teaches Stable Diffusion to accept reference images as prompts by training a small projection on top of a frozen CLIP image encoder that produces cross-attention keys and values with the same semantics as text. The result composes cleanly with ControlNet (one provides structure, the other appearance), supports specialized variants such as a face IP-Adapter trained with face-embedding similarity, and is trainable on (text, reference, target) triplets with augmentations that prevent pixel memorization.
