Datasets & Benchmarks

Section 33.3

Multimodal datasets come in two flavors: the training-scale corpora behind the open models, and the benchmark datasets used for evaluation.

33.3.1 Image training and eval

  • LAION-5B: 5.85B image-text pairs; foundational for open image models. LAION-Aesthetics is the filtered quality subset.
  • DataComp: filtered web image-text corpus from the LAION/Apple/UW collaboration.
  • MS COCO: classic image-captioning benchmark.
  • Conceptual Captions.
  • DrawBench and ImageReward: human-preference image benchmarks.

33.3.2 Video training and eval

  • WebVid: 10M video-text pairs.
  • HD-VILA-100M: high-definition video corpus.
  • VBench: comprehensive video generation benchmark with 16 evaluation dimensions.

33.3.3 Audio and music training and eval

  • AudioSet: 2M+ labeled audio clips.
  • LibriSpeech: standard ASR benchmark.
  • Mozilla Common Voice: multilingual TTS/ASR data.
  • MTG-Jamendo: open music dataset.

Licensing and consent

LAION-5B has faced criticism for inclusion of copyrighted and non-consensually-collected images. Use it for research; check enterprise licenses carefully for commercial training. Synthetic-data alternatives (generated by paid models) sidestep the issue but raise their own license-propagation questions.
