Platforms

Section 33.1

Each modality has its own platform shelf. Image and video generation in particular split sharply between closed APIs (where the SOTA lives) and open-weights (where flexibility and self-hosting live).

33.1.1 Image platforms

  • Midjourney: Discord-first product; SOTA aesthetics, limited API.
  • OpenAI Images (DALL-E 3 / GPT-Image).
  • Google Imagen 4 via AI Studio or Vertex AI.
  • Black Forest Labs (FLUX.1): open weights, also via Replicate and fal.
  • Stability AI (SD3): open weights.
  • Replicate and fal.ai: model-agnostic hosting platforms that resell most open-weights image models behind unified APIs.

33.1.2 Video platforms

  • OpenAI Sora 2: closed API.
  • Google Veo 2 via AI Studio / Vertex.
  • Runway Gen-4: product-led with strong creative tools.
  • Kling: Kuaishou's video model, popular in Asia.
  • Luma Dream Machine.
  • Pika.

33.1.3 Audio and music platforms

  • ElevenLabs: dominant TTS API.
  • Suno v5: dominant music-generation product.
  • Udio: competing music product.
  • Google Lyria.
  • OpenAI Whisper API: ASR.

Aggregators are underrated

Replicate and fal.ai host most open-weights multimodal models behind a uniform API. They are the right answer when you want to A/B test image or video models without managing GPUs.
