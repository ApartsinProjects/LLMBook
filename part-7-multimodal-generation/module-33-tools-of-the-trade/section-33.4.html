Models

Section 33.4

The multimodal model zoo is wider than the text-only zoo. This section enumerates the specific checkpoints worth knowing per modality.

33.4.1 Image generation models

  • Imagen 4 (Google): SOTA closed.
  • GPT-Image / DALL-E 3: OpenAI's image generators.
  • Midjourney v7: best aesthetics by community vote.
  • FLUX.1 [dev / pro / schnell]: open SOTA from Black Forest Labs.
  • Stable Diffusion 3 / SD3.5: Stability AI's open release.

33.4.2 Video generation models

  • Sora 2: OpenAI's flagship.
  • Veo 2: Google DeepMind.
  • Runway Gen-4.
  • Kling.
  • HunyuanVideo (Tencent): open video SOTA.
  • Wan 2.1 (Alibaba): another open video model.

33.4.3 Audio and music models

  • ElevenLabs Multilingual v3: TTS leader.
  • Whisper large-v3: ASR leader.
  • Suno v5: music gen leader.
  • MusicGen: open music gen.
  • Stable Audio Open: open audio gen.

33.4.4 Comparing the models

Multimodal model lifespans are short

SOTA changes every 3 to 6 months in image and video. Build your pipeline to swap models behind an interface; the model you pick this quarter is probably not the model you ship next year.
