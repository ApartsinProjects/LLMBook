Haystack and DSPy: Pipelines and Programmatic Prompting

Section E.3
Stub Section

This section will cover the two more specialized orchestration frameworks. Haystack (by deepset) provides a DAG-based pipeline architecture with typed component I/O, build-time pipeline validation, native OpenTelemetry observability, and YAML pipeline serialization for reproducible production deployments. DSPy (Stanford NLP) replaces hand-crafted prompts with signature-based programs and teleprompter optimizers that compile prompts from labeled examples. The section will include side-by-side recipes for the same retrieval-augmented Q&A task and a discussion of when each framework outperforms LangChain or LlamaIndex.

For the cross-framework comparison and decision tables, see Section E.1. DSPy's signature and optimizer framing is also covered in Chapter 14.5.2 (DSPy: Declarative Prompting with Optimizers).
