Part Overview

The appendices provide reference material organized into two groups. Foundations (Appendix A) covers the math prerequisites every transformer relies on, linear algebra, probability, calculus, and information theory. For Instructors (Appendices B and C) contains five tested course syllabi and per-audience reading pathways. Framework, tooling, and ops material that previously lived in appendices has been consolidated into the part-specific Tools of the Trade chapters so it sits next to the content that uses it.

Big Picture

These appendices serve two purposes: they provide prerequisite refreshers you can consult before diving into specific chapters, and they offer practical framework guides you will return to when building real projects. Think of them as the book's toolbox.
