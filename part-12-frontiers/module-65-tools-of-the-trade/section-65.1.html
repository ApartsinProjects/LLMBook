Platforms

Section 65.1

The platforms for frontier research are paper preprint servers, lab publication channels, and live benchmark leaderboards.

65.1.1 Preprint servers

  • arXiv: the preprint server where almost all LLM research lands first.
  • Semantic Scholar: citation-graph navigation.
  • bioRxiv and medRxiv: for AI applied to bio/medicine.
  • OpenReview: ICLR / NeurIPS / etc. reviews in the open.

65.1.2 Curated paper venues

  • Hugging Face Papers: daily curated arXiv with linked code.
  • Papers with Code: benchmark-linked indexing.
  • alphaXiv: discussion threads on arXiv papers.

65.1.3 Lab publication channels

  • Anthropic research and Transformer Circuits.
  • OpenAI research.
  • Google DeepMind research.
  • EleutherAI publications.
  • Nous Research.
  • Stability AI news.

65.1.4 Live tracking

  • LMArena: human Elo leaderboard.
  • Artificial Analysis: model dashboards.
  • frontier-evals.ai: aggregated frontier-eval scores.
