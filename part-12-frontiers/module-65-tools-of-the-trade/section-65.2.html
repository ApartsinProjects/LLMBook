Libraries & Frameworks

Section 65.2

Libraries for frontier-research workflows are the paper-tracking tools, reproducibility helpers, and the reference-implementation repositories that ship alongside major releases.

65.2.1 Paper-tracking

  • arxiv (Python): API client.
  • Google Scholar with alerts.
  • ResearchRabbit: citation-graph exploration.
  • Elicit: AI-assisted literature review.

65.2.2 Reproducibility libraries

  • Hydra: hierarchical configs.
  • DVC: data version control.
  • Weights & Biases: experiment tracking.
  • Metaflow: research-pipeline framework.

65.2.3 Reference implementations

  • nanoGPT: minimal pretraining reference.
  • llm.c: GPU pretraining in pure C/CUDA.
  • lit-gpt: cleaner from-scratch GPT.
  • EleutherAI/gpt-neox: large-scale pretraining reference.
