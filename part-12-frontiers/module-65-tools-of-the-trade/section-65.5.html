Section 65.5

The final section of the book points at where to keep reading after you finish it. The half-life of any specific tool listed in this book is short; the venues below are the ones that should still be alive when you need to find the next list.

65.5.1 Annual reports

  • State of AI Report (Nathan Benaich, Ian Hogarth).
  • Stanford AI Index.
  • Epoch AI: trends in compute, data, and capability.

  • Ahead of AI (Raschka).

  • The Gradient.

The final practical advice

The field will be different in 18 months. Any tool listed in this chapter may be deprecated, surpassed, or absorbed. What stays constant is the discipline: read papers weekly, build prototypes monthly, measure quarterly, and never confuse the toolbox for the craft. Welcome to the working day.
