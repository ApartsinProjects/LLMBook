Models

Section 65.4

Frontier models are by definition the most recent. This section lists the families pushing the boundary as of 2026, with the caveat that the list will be outdated within months.

65.4.1 Reasoning-first models

  • OpenAI o3 / o-series: dedicated reasoning models with test-time compute scaling.
  • Claude Opus 4.5 with extended thinking.
  • Gemini 2.5 Pro Thinking.
  • DeepSeek-R1: open reasoning model.
  • Qwen QwQ: Alibaba's reasoning preview.

65.4.2 Long-context and multimodal frontier

  • Gemini 2.5 Pro: 2M-token context.
  • Claude Opus 4.5: 1M context.
  • GPT-4o / GPT-5 with multimodal IO.

65.4.3 Open-weights frontier

  • Llama-4, Qwen3, DeepSeek-V4 (covered in Chapter 12). These remain the open shelf in 2026.

65.4.4 Comparing the frontier models

Reasoning is the 2025-2026 frontier

The most important capability change of 2024-2026 was the shift from "predict the next token" models to test-time-compute / reasoning models. The cost-per-task economics changed substantially: more tokens per task, but higher success on the hardest tasks. Whether this trend extends or saturates is the open research question Part XII discusses.
